Pocketbook is a free, open-source travel companion that works completely offline and never tracks you.

<b>No internet permission.</b> The app cannot connect to the network, so nothing you enter can leave your phone unless you share it yourself. No account, no ads, no analytics, no Google Play Services.

<b>Plan</b>
• A route of up to 10 stops across any mix of countries
• Extend your stay and later stops move with it
• Schengen 90/180-day counter
• Passport validity checks against your route
• Entry rules, driving permits and a readiness checklist

<b>On the trip</b>
• Daily brief with sunrise and sunset
• Local and UK clocks with a "good time to call home?" check
• Currency converter using your own rate, and spending in each country's currency with a total in pounds
• Emergency numbers and one-tap Call Home with the correct dialling codes
• "I am here" location to share or show a taxi driver
• Drop a pin at your hotel or car, with a compass to guide you back

<b>Country guide</b>
Plugs and voltage, driving rules, tipping and etiquette, tap water, common scams, medicine rules, phrase cards in large text, a menu decoder and a medical card.

Country packs: Greece, Spain, Italy, France, United States, Japan and Thailand. Designed for travellers from the UK.
