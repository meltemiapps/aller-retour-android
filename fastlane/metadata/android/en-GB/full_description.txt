Aller-Retour is a free, open-source travel companion that works completely offline and never tracks you.

<b>No internet permission.</b> The app cannot connect to the network, so nothing you enter can leave your phone unless you share it yourself. No account, no ads, no analytics, no Google Play Services.

<b>Plan</b>
• A route of up to 10 stops across any mix of countries
• Extend your stay and later stops move with it
• Schengen 90/180-day counter
• Passport validity checks against your route
• Entry rules, driving permits and a readiness checklist

<b>On the trip</b>
• Today: where you are, sunset, time difference, quick convert and what's coming up
• Currency converter using your own rate, and spending in each country's currency with a total in pounds
• Emergency numbers and one-tap Call Home with the correct dialling codes
• "I am here" location as text, ready to read out or send
• Drop a pin at your hotel or car, then see how far it is and which way
• Optional reminders, set on the phone only

<b>Country guide</b>
Safety and emergencies, plugs and voltage, driving rules, tipping and etiquette, tap water, common scams, medicine rules, phrase cards in large text, a menu decoder and a medical card.

<b>Looks the way you like</b>
Glass cards over a choice of backgrounds (sky, sunset, olive grove, night harbour) or one of your own photos, which stays on your phone.

Country packs: Greece, Spain, Italy, France, United States, Japan and Thailand. Designed for travellers from the UK.
