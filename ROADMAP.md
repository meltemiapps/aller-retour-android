# Roadmap

Aller-Retour stays free, open source and offline. A feature that needs a server ships only as an **opt-in** with no identifiers, or not at all.

## Version 1 (this release)
Multi-stop route with extend-my-stay, Schengen counter, passport checks, readiness list, converter with your own rate, multi-currency expenses, emergency numbers, Call Home, "I am here", Drop a pin with compass, country guide, phrase cards, menu decoder, medical card, packing lists, backup and restore.

## Version 2: the journey
- Booking wallet (flights, hotels, ferries, trains) with free-cancellation countdowns and a Plan B for each booking
- Import a booking by sharing a confirmation email or PDF to the app, parsed on the device
- Connections check: tight check-out-to-flight gaps, early arrivals, ferry weather risk, legs with no transport booked
- Reminders scheduled on the device: check-in opens, leave for the airport, check-out, cancellation deadlines
- Return-home countdown and medication times across time zones
- Safety check-in timer with a prepared SMS alert
- Document vault encrypted with Android Keystore and unlocked with fingerprint
- Lost card, passport and phone playbook; flight delay rights (UK261/EU261); incident log and claim pack
- Group cost split, budget by stop, trip wrap-up
- Business mode: meetings in local and UK time, daily allowances, receipts, CSV expense report

## Version 3: memories and reach
- Offline maps (MapLibre with downloadable OpenStreetMap regions)
- Photo map from local EXIF data, trip journal and PDF scrapbook export
- Itinerary sharing by QR code
- Optional, opt-in exchange-rate and weather refresh from no-key public sources (ECB reference rates, Open-Meteo). This would be a separate build flavour so the default app keeps no internet permission
- More country packs and home countries other than the UK
- Translations (move UI strings into `strings.xml`)
- Encrypted backups
