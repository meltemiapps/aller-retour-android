Pocketbook
Zero-tracking travel companion

Covers the whole journey: planning, travelling, changes of plan and getting home safely. Everything stays on the device, with no account, cloud or analytics.

  • Today has the daily brief, next event, reminders, medication times, return countdown and the dual clock.
  • Plan covers holiday or business mode, a route of up to 10 stops with "extend my stay", the Schengen 90/180 counter, the booking wallet with free-cancel countdowns, connection checks and Plan B, document expiry and entry rules, readiness, home prep, meetings and a QR itinerary.
  • Money has the converter, group cost split, budget by stop and the trip wrap-up.
  • Safety has SOS numbers, the check-in timer, Call Home, "I Am Here", Drop a Pin, the lost-item playbook, flight delay rights, the incident log and "I'm home".
  • Guide covers power, driving, etiquette, water, medication rules, phrases, the menu decoder, the medical card and UK customs allowances.
  • Kit has packing, the vault, the journal, the map and backup.

Use the ◀ ▶ arrows in the Today tab to step through the trip day by day. Rates, weather, GPS and the compass are simulated. Reminders show what the phone would schedule on-device. Photos stay in memory for this session only. Rules and allowances are summaries, so check official sources before you travel.
